### Recommendation
- **Shipped box: Arka custom mailer, outside print, 10-pack ($3.57/box).** It's the cheapest *printed, pretty* option with no setup fee, full color, US-made, and you can order 10 to test. Packlane is the backup ($3.40 shown, but it varies by size).
- **Pickup box: Uline S-4310 plain kraft ($0.70/box, 25 per bundle)** plus a $10–15 custom rubber stamp and twine. It looks hand-made on purpose and costs very little.
- Skip magnetic boxes for v1. At $9+ each they cost more than the lab.

## Selling rules

### eBay: physical kit with digital access included
- eBay's *Electronically delivered items* policy says only eBay-approved sellers can list electronically delivered goods outside **Everything Else > Information Products** (classified-ad format). Others must use that category. Local pickup isn't allowed for electronically delivered goods. Sellers must say what the buyer needs to get access (for example, making an account) and that the buyer gets a **revocable license**, not ownership. Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/digitally-delivered-goods-policy?id=4289
- How to frame it: list the **physical STEM kit** (box, lab, magazine, card, shirt) in a toys/education category. Say the app access card is included, and add the license and account-requirement disclosures in the description. Don't list the app subscription by itself. The listing must not depend on emailing a code.
- If any lab items count as chemicals or hazmat, check eBay's hazardous-materials rules and the carrier rules. A dry lab avoids that.

### Facebook Marketplace: sell the physical kit only
- Meta lists **"Digital products or subscriptions"** as not allowed on Marketplace (also: animals). Source: https://www.meta.com/safety/scam-prevention/marketplace-safety/ (Commerce Policies: https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce)
- Services and lessons also aren't allowed as Marketplace items under the Commerce Policies. Don't list tutoring or classes there.
- How to frame it: "Farm STEM kit: mason-jar vacuum lab, blocks, stamps, activity magazine. Includes a free app access card." The price is for the physical kit, and the app is a bonus inside.

### Google reviews (applies to every tier)
- No rewards of any kind for reviews, including discounts, free items, spins, shirts or levels. Don't ask only happy customers for reviews. Asking for an honest review with no reward is fine. Source: https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en
