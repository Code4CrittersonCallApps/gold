Privacy Policy

Critters on Call Gold · Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version: Gold has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal information. The little bit it remembers (like whether you already spun the wheel this month) stays on your own phone or tablet.

Who we are

Gold is made by Sheehan Homestead LLC, which runs Critters on Call, a family farm and mobile petting zoo in Callahan, Florida. Gold is a free app for kids and families with farm games, animal lessons and member perks.

What the app collects

Nothing. Gold does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information. There is no sign-up and no login. Gold has no analytics, no advertising, no ad tracking and no third-party trackers, and it does not send any information from your device to us.

What stays on your device

Gold uses your browser's local storage to remember a few simple things, such as which month you last used each prize wheel and game progress or scores. This information stays on your device only. We never see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or your browser data, or by uninstalling the app.

Gold also saves a copy of its own pages and pictures on your device so it can open without a signal. That copy contains no personal information.

Sharing and gift links

When you tap Share, Gold opens your phone's own share menu or copies a link for you. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it yourself, and the app never sees who you send it to. If you type a name to send a friend a spin, that name goes into the link you share. It is not sent to us or stored by us.

Fonts and links to other pages

To show its text style, Gold loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, Google's servers receive the technical details needed to deliver the font file (such as your device's IP address and browser type). Gold does not send Google any other information. You can read Google's privacy policy.

Some buttons open other pages, such as our games in Critters Play, our booking pages, or sheehanhomestead.com. Phone and text buttons open your phone's own dialer or messaging app. If you book a visit or contact us, we only receive the information you choose to give us, and we use it only to answer you and arrange your visit.

Kids

Gold is made for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13. Parents: any booking, purchase or contact with us happens on separate pages and is meant to be done by an adult.

Permissions

Gold does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Changes

If we ever change how Gold handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
